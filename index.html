Two fighting traditions collide. Move, block with distance, and time your strikes to win the round.

PLAYER 1 · SHAOLIN

A / D to move, W to jump, F for a fast strike, G for a powerful but slower strike.

PLAYER 2 · WUTANG

Arrow keys to move, ↑ to jump, K for a fast strike, L for a powerful strike. Turn CPU off for two-player mode.
